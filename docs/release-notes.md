# Lid Switch 1.2

Turn the illuminated Apple logo on your MacBook Pro on or off with one click. While Lid Switch is running, its window, Dock icon and menu bar icon show the same setting.

**Requires an Apple silicon MacBook Pro with a built-in PROLED XDR display, running macOS 14 or later.** [Check your display](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149248)

Download **Lid-Switch.dmg**, open it and drag **Lid Switch** into **Applications**. Open the app and click the light. macOS asks for administrator approval to install a helper for your account. After that, you can switch the light from the window or menu bar without entering your password.

**Lid-Switch.zip** contains the same app. **SHA256SUMS** lists the checksums for both downloads.

To uninstall, choose **Lid Switch → Remove Background Control…**, quit and move the app to the Trash.
