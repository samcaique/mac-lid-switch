# Lid Switch 1.2

Click the light in the window or menu bar to switch the illuminated logo on your MacBook Pro. The Dock and menu bar icons show the current setting while the app is running.

**Requires an Apple silicon MacBook Pro with a built-in PROLED XDR display and macOS 14 or later.** [Check your display](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149248)

[Download Lid Switch for Mac](https://github.com/samcaique/mac-lid-switch/releases/download/v1.2.0/Lid-Switch.dmg). Open the disk image, drag **Lid Switch** into **Applications**, and open it. On the first click, macOS asks for administrator approval to install the helper. After that, one click switches the light.

The downloads use an ad hoc signature and have not been notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the app, [install from source](https://github.com/samcaique/mac-lid-switch/tree/v1.2.0#build-from-source).

**Lid-Switch.zip** contains the same app. **Lid-Switch-source.zip** contains the source, tests and artwork. **SHA256SUMS** lists the checksums for all three files.

To uninstall, choose **Lid Switch → Remove Background Control…**, quit the app, then move it to the Trash.
